Privacy Policy for Japan Swissknife

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Privacy

Japan Swissknife is designed as an offline-first travel toolkit. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

Locally Stored Data

Some features allow users to enter and save information on their device, such as travel information, emergency information, addresses, favourites, and personal notes.

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Internet Access

The core functionality of Japan Swissknife is designed to work offline. Some features may open external websites or services selected by the user. Those external services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Advertising and Tracking

Japan Swissknife does not contain advertising and does not use advertising identifiers, analytics trackers, or behavioural tracking.

Medical and Emergency Information

Medical, emergency, translation, and travel information provided by Japan Swissknife is intended as a practical communication and reference aid. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, emergency, or government advice.

Children's Privacy

Japan Swissknife does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's functionality changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Japan Swissknife Google Play listing.
